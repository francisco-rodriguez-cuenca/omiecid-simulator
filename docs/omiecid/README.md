# Certification evidence

This directory holds the research evidence behind the simulator's market rules
and its certification. For a short introduction, read the
[market model](../market-model.md) and the
[modelling assumptions](../modelling-assumptions.md) first.

| Document | Content |
|---|---|
| [market-spec.md](market-spec.md) | The market specification: rules of the Iberian continuous intraday market, each with its regulatory source |
| [open-questions.md](open-questions.md) | Ambiguities in the sources and their rulings (OQ-nn) |
| [scenario-ladder.md](scenario-ladder.md) | The certification scenarios: purpose, dataset, known result, pass criterion |
| [scenario-ladder-review.md](scenario-ladder-review.md) | The domain review of the scenarios, with its rulings and findings |
| [certificate.md](certificate.md) | The certificate: one section per scenario, plus conformance gaps, engineering limits and residual risk |
| [certification-log.md](certification-log.md) | The verdict log of the certification runs |
| [corpus/](corpus/README.md) | Text of the two OMIE documents that may be redistributed, and references to the two that may not |

**Four documents are verbatim; two are regenerated.**
[market-spec.md](market-spec.md), [open-questions.md](open-questions.md),
[scenario-ladder.md](scenario-ladder.md) and
[scenario-ladder-review.md](scenario-ladder-review.md) are kept byte for byte as
written in the research project, apart from the paths that point to the
regulatory corpus. The certification notebooks
([notebooks/certification/](../../notebooks/certification/README.md)) check every
regulatory quote they rely on against these files by digest, so they must not be
edited. [certificate.md](certificate.md) and
[certification-log.md](certification-log.md) are regenerated by this release's
own re-run of the certification notebooks; some tests read them, so they are not
edited by hand either, apart from the dated editorial amendments recorded in the
certificate's Appendix A. See [Validation](../validation.md) for how the evidence
fits together.

**Public identifiers used in these documents and in the notebooks:**

- **OQ-nn** — a modelling ruling or open question in
  [open-questions.md](open-questions.md), for example OQ-31.
- **R-n** — a ruling of the scenario review (R-1, R-2), and **V3-nn** — a
  finding of the third pass of that review, in
  [scenario-ladder-review.md](scenario-ladder-review.md). Findings of its later
  passes are labelled D3-nn.
- **Cnn** — a certification scenario. The notebooks and the certificate write
  C00 to C16; the ladder specification writes the numbers without the leading
  zero (C0, C1, …).

**What reads differently in a verbatim document.** The four verbatim documents
mention `gymnax_exchange`, the former package root of the same code (now
`omiecid_simulator`). They also mention internal paths, documents and work items
of the research repository, and contain links to research files that are not
part of this release, so those links do not resolve here.
