1. BACKGROUND 
 
The new version of the Iberian market rules, so called “Reglas de funcionamiento de los 
mercados diario e intradiario de electricidad” (from now on “Reglas” or simply “rules”), it 
has been prepared for the entering into production of the new pan european intraday 
auctions (IDAs) and was approved by the spanish NRA (CNMC) through “Resolución de 
la Comisión Nacional de los Mercados y la Competencia” from last 23 May 2024. 
 
In such approval, the CNMC stated: 
 
 “La presente resolución surtirá efectos en la fecha de puesta en producción de las 
subastas intradiarias europeas, a determinar por el proyecto de acoplamiento 
intradiario europeo (Single Intraday Coupling -SIDC-), y que será comunicada por 
el operador del mercado a la CNMC, a los agentes de mercado y a los operadores 
de sistema tan pronto haya sido determinada.” 
 
The Single Intraday Coupling pro ject (SIDC) has set the date f or IDA’s Go-live by the 
next 13 June 2024, with delivery day 14 June 2024 (D+1). 
 
Last 29 May 2024, OMIE sent to all market participants an email informing them all about 
the date for IDA’s Go-live. 
 
The purpose of this “Instrucción 1/2024” is to detail under normal circumstances, the 
market operations  expected during next 13 June  2024, in order to allow market 
participants to know in advance (and to be prepared) for the actions needed to be taken 
for putting in production this new IDA market. 
 
2. SIXTH REGIONAL INTRADAY AUCTION  SESSION DURING NEXT  
13/06/204 (DELIVERY DAY 13/06/2024)

The Sixth Regional Intraday Auction Session (MI6) during next 13/06/2024 (delivery day 
also 13/06/204) will have open its orders submission window from 09:00h till 09:50  
normally. 
 
This MI6 session  will not be affected by the IDA ’s Go-live and it will be  the very last 
session of the Complementary Regional Intraday Auctions (CRIDAs) in Iberia. 
 
3. INTRADAY CONTINUOUS MARKET ROUNDS DURING NEXT 13/06/204 
(until 13:00h, starting of Round 16th) 
 
Under normal operations, rounds of the Intraday Continuous Market (MIC) for the trading 
contracts of 13/06/2024 will keep on trading without any interruption until 13:00h, when 
local IDA maintenance starts (needed at OMIE for IDA’s Go-live). 
 
4. DAY AHEAD SESSION  WITH DELIVERY DAY  14/06/204 ( OPERATED 
DURING 13/06/2024) 
 
Day-Ahead market session with delivery day 14/06/2024, will reach its Gate Closure 
Time (GCT) at 12:00h (13/06/2024) normally.  
 
Under normal operations, this Day-Ahead market session will not be impacted by IDA’s 
Go-live. 
 
Local maintenance window will start just after publication of Day -Ahead results, 
approximately at 13:00h. All details of this maintenance will be communicated in advance 
to all market participant as usual through OMIE’s market website as usual. 
 
5. LOCAL MAINTENANCE AT OMIE BEFORE IDA’s GO-LIVE. 
 
Starting at 13:00h (aprox), once the Day-Ahead market session has finished without any 
impact on  its normal operational timings, OMIE will perform the local maintenance 
needed for the IDA’s Go-live. 
 
Maintenance window will last approximately 60 minutes, expecting to be finished earlier 
than IDA1 Gate Opening Time (GOT) which usually takes place at 14:00h  just after 
successful integration of PDBF/PT at OMIE.

During the maintenance window, continuous trading (MIC) will be halted until 
maintenance is over (affecting to Round 16th). 
 
Once the maintenance is finished, the new market rules will fully apply. Thus, all orders 
hibernated in XBID because of the maintenance will remain hibernated after it ends and 
the trading starts  again, requiring the manual activation of such orders  by the related 
market participant. 
 
6. FIRST IDA SESSION (IDA1) FOR DELIVERY DAY 14/06/204 ( OPERATED 
DURING 13/06/2024, FROM 14:00H TO 15:20H) 
 
At 14:00h 13/06/2024, once the maintenance has finished and both PDBF and PDBFPT 
have been integrated at OMIE’s local systems without any issues found, IDA1 GOT, with 
trading schedule for periods 1 to 24, will take place at OMIE. 
 
New market rules will  fully apply already including the new complex order typology 
(simple block orders), which can be already used from this IDA1 on.  
 
If there are no further issues, Gate Closure Time (GCT) of IDA1 will take place at 15:00h 
13/06/2024. Market participants can follow Annex1 of the market rules to know the most 
important timings of the intraday markets (IDAs and continuous). 
 
On the other hand, IDA1 preliminary results are expected to be published at 15:18h, 
being the results confirmed by all partes (finals) at 15:20h. 
 
7. INTRADAY CONTINUOUS MARKET ROUNDS FOR NEXT 14/06/204 (MIC 
GOT) 
 
If no further issues are found, continuous trading for delivery day 14/06/2024 will open 
at 15:20h (13/06/2024) starting with Round 18th, after successful completion of IDA1. 
 
For further details about MIC rounds (sequence & timings), please check Annex 1 of the 
market rules.

10. EXCEPCIONAL SITUATIONS 
 
Due to the broad scope and complexity of this new IDA market, and considering the high 
number of parties involved, there is risk of potential issues impacting on Go-live expected

timings, creating exceptional situations that could obliged parties to deviate from the 
initial plan established for IDA’s Go-live. 
 
If such issues unfortunately take place, parties will follow the corresponding operational 
procedures from SDAC ( Single Day Ahead Coupling ) and/or SIDC ( Single Intraday 
Coupling) if they apply, being every new situation communicated to all market 
participants by using the usual communication channels at OMIE’s market website as 
soon as possible. 
 
Listed below are the most likely cases of delays and their potential consequences: 
 
a) Day-Ahead incidents and/or delays.  
 
 
If, for any reason, Day-Ahead market session suffers delays with respect to the normal 
operation and publication times, the maintenance window necessary to implement IDAs 
that takes place after Day-Ahead completion would inevitably be delayed as well. 
 
Consequently, this delay could delay IDA1 GOT beyond 14:00h. In the worst case, this 
situation could lead into a cancellation of MIBEL's participation during the first IDA1 if the 
cascade of delays makes it unavoidable. 
 
b) Local maintenance incidents and/or delays.  
 
If the local maintenance encounters problems that cause delays with respect to the 
planned timing, the subsequent intraday processes will inevitably be delayed as well, 
and the IDA1  GOT will take place later than 14:00h.  In the worst case,  this situation 
could lead to a cancellation of MIBEL's participation during the first IDA1 if such delay 
makes it unavoidable. 
 
c) Delays in TSOs publications after Day-Ahead.  
 
Both Iberian TSOs must change their local systems to a dapt themselves to the new 
intraday market, in the same way than OMIE does . As a result, it could be delays on 
TSOs side during PDBF/PT and PDVP/PT publication. 
 
In such a case and in coordination with both TSOs, the emergency operation sequence 
described in rule "59.3 ALTERACIONES AL HORARIO" will be followed, communicating
