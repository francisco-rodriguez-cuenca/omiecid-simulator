# Regulatory corpus

This directory holds plain-text extractions of two public documents published
by OMIE (Operador del Mercado Ibérico de Energía, Polo Español, S.A.) at
www.omie.es. They are included only so that the quote checks of the
certification evidence can be verified against the source text.

**These files are not part of OmieCID-Simulator and are not covered by its
Apache-2.0 licence.** They are reproduced under OMIE's website legal warning
(<https://www.omie.es/en/legal-warning>), which allows free use of its public,
free-of-charge information provided that the original content is fully
respected and the source is cited. Source: OMIE. OMIE has not endorsed this
project.

## Shipped documents

Both files are in
`Mapping_Authoritative_Documents_EU_Continuous_Intraday_Trading_SIDC_XBID_Spain_OMIE/`.
Each is a machine text extraction of the PDF (page by page, under a short
header giving the source URL), otherwise unmodified. Extraction artefacts such
as broken words or spacing may occur; the PDF at the source URL is the
reference.

| File | Publisher | Title | Source | Version date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `OMIE_Day-Ahead_and_Intraday_Electricity_Market_Rules_English.md` | OMIE | Day-Ahead and Intraday Electricity Market Rules (English) | <https://www.omie.es/sites/default/files/2025-03/r_28022025_en.pdf> | 28 February 2025 |
| `OMIE_Instruction_1_2024_IDA_Go-Live_Operations.md` | OMIE | Instrucción 1/2024: Market operations during next 13 June 2024, go-live day of the pan-European intraday auctions (IDA), English version 2.0 | <https://www.omie.es/sites/default/files/2024-06/Instruccion_1_2024_EN_v2_0.pdf> | 7 June 2024 |

The market rules file is OMIE's **non-binding** English translation. As the
document itself states, it is not binding on the Market Operator, and in the
event of any discrepancy the Spanish version prevails, under the terms of the
CNMC Resolution of 27 February 2025 (DCOOR/DE/009/24), published in Spain's
Official State Gazette (BOE) on 28 February 2025.
