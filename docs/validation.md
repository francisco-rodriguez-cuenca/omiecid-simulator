# Validation

The matching engine is **certified** against a ladder of 17 scenarios, C00–C16.
All 17 are CERTIFIED (certificate dated 2026-10-26, amended 2026-10-27 and
2026-10-29). The `Session` built on it is **verified** by a byte-identical
parity check against the research environment it comes from: the
reinforcement-learning environment of the research project this package was
extracted from (not part of this release). This page explains both and where
the evidence lives.

## The certification ladder

Each scenario isolates one behaviour of the market, states the rule it certifies
(quoted from the [market specification](omiecid/market-spec.md) or a recorded
ruling in [open-questions.md](omiecid/open-questions.md)), builds its own data
with the synthetic tape generator, and compares the engine against a known
result. The scenarios and what each one covers are specified in
[scenario-ladder.md](omiecid/scenario-ladder.md).

### Three kinds of evidence

A scenario combines:

- **goldens**: the data is drawn as the parameters of a construction whose
  outcome is a closed form in those parameters, so the expected result is
  computed independently of the engine;
- **conservation invariants**: accounting identities that must hold after every
  run, whatever the input (every MW submitted ends filled, resting, or cancelled
  or expired; volume bought equals volume sold; cash sums to zero across
  participants), checked by a common invariant battery;
- **metamorphic invariants**: required relations between two related runs (for
  example, relabelling which participant owns which order must not change the
  trade list, because OMIE has no self-trade prevention).

Every scenario runs the engine twice, eagerly and compiled with `jax.jit`, and
requires the two records to be identical. Each scenario also has an
independently written audit test under `tests/omiecid/certification/`.
[scenario-ladder.md](omiecid/scenario-ladder.md) explains the method in full,
including what the combination does not cover.

### The evidence documents

The evidence directory, [`docs/omiecid/`](omiecid/README.md), holds two
documents regenerated by this release's own certification re-run:

- [certificate.md](omiecid/certificate.md): one signed section per scenario,
  the conformance gaps, the engineering limits and the residual risk;
- [certification-log.md](omiecid/certification-log.md): every certification
  run, in order;

and four documents the notebooks check their quotes against, kept verbatim
(apart from the paths to the regulatory corpus):

- [market-spec.md](omiecid/market-spec.md): the market rules the engine
  implements;
- [open-questions.md](omiecid/open-questions.md): the recorded rulings
  (`OQ-nn`) where the regulation is silent or ambiguous;
- [scenario-ladder.md](omiecid/scenario-ladder.md) and
  [scenario-ladder-review.md](omiecid/scenario-ladder-review.md): the ladder
  specification and its domain review (`R-n`, `V3-nn`).

The [corpus/](omiecid/corpus/README.md) holds the OMIE source documents that can
be redistributed. The identifiers, and what reads differently in the verbatim
documents, are explained in the [evidence directory guide](omiecid/README.md).

### The notebooks

The 17 notebooks are in [`notebooks/certification/`](../notebooks/certification/README.md),
one per scenario, all with the same nine sections: purpose, the rule quoted,
configuration, dataset, known result, run, certification, verdict and, on
failure, an explanation. Notebooks are stored without outputs.

The results of the release run (one JSON file per scenario) are not committed.
They are published as the pre-release
[`certification-results-2026-10`](https://github.com/francisco-rodriguez-cuenca/omiecid-simulator/releases/tag/certification-results-2026-10);
[Testing](testing.md#certification-results) describes them and how the audit
tests use them.

## Re-running a notebook

Each notebook runs interactively in Jupyter or headless, exactly as the test
suite runs it. The commands are in the notebooks README, section
[Running a notebook](../notebooks/certification/README.md#running-a-notebook).

## The Session

The `Session` is the research environment with its reinforcement-learning
layers (action decoding, rewards, observations) removed.
Its mechanics are unchanged, so it is **verified rather than certified**: a
parity harness drives the `Session` and the research environment with the same
inputs and compares their outputs and states byte for byte, step by step,
eagerly, under `jax.jit` and under `jax.vmap`. The one intended difference is
the self-match fix: the `Session` also attributes the passive side of a
participant's self-match (see [Sessions](sessions.md#self-trades)), and on those
steps the harness checks the difference against the matched legs exactly. The
engine source was checked to be identical to the certified one. Three
scenarios, C00, C13 and C16, run through the `Session` and were re-run for this
release.

The `Session` contract itself (stamping, the `FillReport`, gate closure, cash,
saturation) is covered by `tests/omiecid/test_session.py` and its independent
audit twin `tests/omiecid/test_session_audit.py`; self-trade accounting by
`tests/omiecid/test_session_self_trade.py` and its twin. See
[Limitations](limitations.md).
